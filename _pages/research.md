## Research Overview

My research focuses on developing safe, intelligent, and adaptive control and decision-making frameworks for autonomous robotic systems operating under uncertainty, limited sensing, and safety constraints.

I combine nonlinear control theory, reinforcement learning, Gaussian processes, and uncertainty-aware planning to address challenges in autonomous navigation, safe exploration, robotic sensing, and trajectory tracking.

My research spans both theoretical algorithm development and simulation-based and experimental validation.

---

## Research Areas

### 1. Safe and Intelligent Autonomous Robotics

I am interested in designing autonomous robotic systems that can safely navigate, explore, and collect information in uncertain environments.

Key research topics include:

- Safe and informative path planning for mobile robots.
- Safety-constrained exploration and autonomous sampling.
- Gaussian process-based scalar field estimation.
- Motion planning under sensing and communication constraints.
- Uncertainty-aware decision-making and trajectory optimization.

My work includes developing reinforcement learning-based sampling policies that balance information acquisition with safety requirements.

### 2. Gaussian Processes and Uncertainty-Aware Learning

Gaussian processes provide probabilistic models for learning unknown environments and estimating spatially varying quantities.

My work investigates:

- Gaussian process regression with uncertain or biased inputs.
- Scalar field mapping and environmental estimation.
- Confidence-based safe exploration.
- Safety-aware mapping in hazardous environments.
- Uncertainty quantification for robotic planning and control.

I am particularly interested in combining Gaussian process models with planning and control algorithms to improve autonomous operation in uncertain environments.

### 3. Nonlinear, Adaptive, and Safety-Critical Control

My research includes adaptive and nonlinear control methods for robotic systems with uncertain dynamics and incomplete state measurements.

Research topics include:

- Adaptive and robust nonlinear control.
- Safety-critical control under state and input constraints.
- Observer-based state estimation.
- Switched adaptive control for trajectory tracking.
- Control under intermittent measurements and GPS-denied conditions.

During my 2024 AFRL research internship, I developed a switched adaptive observer and control framework to reduce sensing requirements while maintaining trajectory-tracking performance.

### 4. Reinforcement Learning and UAV Control

I investigate learning-based control approaches for autonomous systems, particularly when conventional model-based control approaches face uncertainty or system failures.

Research topics include:

- Safe reinforcement learning for autonomous robots.
- Actor-critic and policy-gradient methods.
- Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and Advantage Actor-Critic (A2C).
- Fault-tolerant control of quadcopters.
- Reinforcement learning-based gliding control.
- UAV stability, navigation, and flight control.

My previous work includes fault-tolerant quadcopter control under propeller failure and reinforcement learning-based gliding control.
