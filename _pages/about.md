<p><strong>Ph.D. Researcher — Robotics, Autonomous Systems, and Control</strong></p>

I am a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Florida, working under the supervision of Dr. Rushikesh Kamalapurkar. I expect to graduate in May 2027.

My research focuses on autonomous robotic systems, nonlinear and safety-critical control, reinforcement learning, and Gaussian processes. I am particularly interested in developing safe, intelligent, and uncertainty-aware decision-making and control algorithms for autonomous systems operating in challenging environments.

My work includes safe exploration, informative path planning, GPS-denied navigation, adaptive control, and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs). I have also conducted robotics research through collaborations with the Air Force Research Laboratory (AFRL).

Prior to joining the University of Florida, I earned my M.S. in Aerospace Engineering from Air University and my B.E. in Aerospace Engineering from NUST, Pakistan.
