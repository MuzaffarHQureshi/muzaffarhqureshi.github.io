I presented my paper, **"A Switched Adaptive Control Approach to Reduce Sensing Needs in Trajectory Tracking Problems,"** at the **2026 American Control Conference (ACC)** in New Orleans, Louisiana.

This work, published in *IEEE Control Systems Letters*, studies switched adaptive control methods for reducing sensing requirements while maintaining trajectory-tracking performance under intermittent state measurements.

ACC 2026 was a valuable opportunity to present my work, interact with researchers in control and robotics, and engage in technical discussions with the broader control community.
