# Muzaffar H Qureshi — Academic Website

Personal academic website and research portfolio of **Muzaffar H Qureshi**, a Ph.D. researcher in Mechanical Engineering at the University of Florida.

**Website:** https://muzaffarhqureshi.github.io/

## About Me

I am a Ph.D. researcher in the Department of Mechanical and Aerospace Engineering at the University of Florida, working under the supervision of Dr. Rushikesh Kamalapurkar.

**Expected Graduation:** May 2027

My research focuses on robotics, autonomous systems, nonlinear and safety-critical control, reinforcement learning, and Gaussian processes.

## Research Interests

- Autonomous Robotics and Intelligent Systems
- Nonlinear, Adaptive, and Safety-Critical Control
- Reinforcement Learning and Safe Exploration
- Gaussian Processes and Uncertainty Quantification
- Informative Path Planning and GPS-Denied Navigation
- UAV Guidance, Navigation, and Control

## Website Sections

- **Home:** Academic profile and research introduction
- **Education:** Academic qualifications, awards, and certifications
- **Research:** Research interests and experience
- **Publications:** Journal articles, conference papers, and preprints
- **Projects:** Selected robotics and control projects
- **CV:** Curriculum vitae
- **News:** Conferences, academic visits, collaborations, and research highlights

## Website Technology

This website is built using Jekyll and the Academic Pages template and hosted on GitHub Pages.

Publications are generated from BibTeX bibliographies using Jekyll Scholar. Deployment is automated through GitHub Actions.
